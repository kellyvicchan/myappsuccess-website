Our approach

Most students have the potential to be outstanding candidates. They just don't know it yet.

Honest guidance about the process, no sugarcoating

We teach you what colleges actually reward and cut through the generic, play-it-safe advice other programs hand out. With real industry insight, we help you think for yourself and shape a personal strategy with the self-awareness to get where you want to go.

Other programs prep applications. We prep candidates.

Our framework does more than help you navigate the process and sound good on paper. It's designed to develop you, throughout the process and long after college. The focus isn't instruction, it's empowerment, because you are more capable and interesting than you think.

FAQ

Common questions

About the bootcamp and private sessions.

Who is the bootcamp designed for?
The bootcamp is built for high school juniors, rising seniors and seniors who need support fast. It also works well for international students, competitive program applicants and anyone who wants to put their strongest application forward.
Are we guaranteed admission to our college of choice?
We position you as your best version to be evaluated by colleges, and our framework is built to improve that positioning. No one can guarantee which school you will get into.
What's the difference between the bootcamp and private sessions?
The bootcamp is a structured 6-week group program with weekly workshops, community access and resources. Private sessions are standalone services you book individually for targeted help, such as an essay review or a strategy call. Many students do both.
What happens after the 6-week bootcamp ends?
Students keep full access to the resources library and the community after the program ends, so the connections and tools you build stay with you throughout your application journey.
Do you work with students who aren't applying to Ivy League schools?
Absolutely. We work with students applying to all types of schools, including state universities, liberal arts colleges, women's colleges and international programs. Every student deserves a strong application, wherever they're aiming.
Can we do a payment plan?
We offer payment plans for the bootcamp but not for private sessions. Apply to the bootcamp and we'll reach out with payment plan options.
Are merit scholarships available?
Yes. We offer merit scholarships for qualifying students. Reach out through the bootcamp application form and tell us about your situation. Cost should not be a barrier to quality guidance.
Is all the work done by humans?
Yes, 100%. Every review, session and piece of feedback comes from a real human expert. We do not use AI to write or evaluate your materials.
I'm from an agency or education business. Can we partner?
We'd love to talk. Visit our page for education brands or email marketing@myappsuccess.com to set up an intro call. We reply within 1 business day.
