Our Approach

Most students have the potential to be outstanding candidates, they just don't know it

01

We Are Advisors Who Have Been There

Our founders navigated U.S. college admissions alone, with no resources or family guidance. They went on to win national scholarships (QuestBridge, Burger King, Rotary Club), earn degrees from Cornell and Johns Hopkins, and work at companies like Apple, Accenture, Adobe, Goldman Sachs, and Harvard. We built MyAppSuccess so every student has access to the roadmap we had to piece together ourselves, tested through private sessions that have led to admissions at 113 colleges, with mentors bringing 22 years of combined experience from Harvard, Cornell, Johns Hopkins, MIT, Stanford, and UPenn.

02

Honest Guidance About The Process, No Sugarcoating

We teach you what colleges actually reward and cut through the generic, play-it-safe advice other programs hand out. With real industry insight, we help you think for yourself and shape a personal strategy with the self-awareness to get to where you want to go.

03

Other Programs Prep Applications, We Prep Candidates

Our framework does more than help you navigate the application process and sound good on paper. It's designed to develop you, throughout the process and long after college. The focus isn't instruction, it's empowerment. Because you are more capable and interesting than you think.
